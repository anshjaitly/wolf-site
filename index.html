Wolf

A personal study and to-do assistant

Wolf is a small personal assistant that runs on its developer's own Mac. It keeps a to-do list, sends reminders, and helps with studying. With permission, it can read its developer's own Google Calendar and Gmail to point out schedule clashes and email deadlines, and it can send an email only after its developer approves that specific email.

Wolf is not a public service. It is used by one person, with their own Google account. There is nothing to sign up for.
