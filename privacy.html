Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Wolf is a personal assistant used by its developer, with the developer's own Google account. This policy explains what Wolf does with Google user data.

What Wolf accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only): event times and titles, to show the day's schedule and point out clashes.
  • Gmail (read-only): recent message senders, subjects and short previews, to point out emails that may need a reply or mention a deadline.
  • Gmail (send): Wolf sends an email only when the developer writes it and then approves that exact email with a "yes". It never sends on its own.

How the data is used and stored

  • Google data is processed on the developer's own Mac and shown only to the developer.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain on that Mac, not in files and not on any server.
  • Wolf does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.
  • Wolf does not send Gmail or Calendar data to AI services, and it is not used to train any model.

Limited Use

Wolf's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's sign-in from the Mac also removes the stored tokens.
